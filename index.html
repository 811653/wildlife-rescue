About Wildlife Rescue

Wildlife Rescue is dedicated to helping injured, orphaned, and displaced wildlife. Our goal is to provide animals with the care they need and, whenever possible, return them safely to their natural habitat.

Our Mission

Our mission is to protect local wildlife through rescue, rehabilitation, education, and conservation. We believe every animal deserves compassionate care and a chance to return to the wild.

How You Can Help

You can make a difference by volunteering, supporting wildlife conservation, donating supplies, or helping educate others about protecting wildlife.
